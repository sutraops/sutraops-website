Learning Path 01

SAP FICO Learning

Build an end-to-end understanding of SAP FI and CO, including enterprise structure, configuration concepts, integration, month-end activities and practical interview preparation.

Explore the roadmapAll learning paths

Suggested learning roadmap

  • 01 · SAP and enterprise structureSAP architecture, clients, company codes, charts of accounts, fiscal year variants and posting periods.
  • 02 · General Ledger and core FIGL master data, document types, number ranges, posting keys, field status, journals and ledgers.
  • 03 · AP, AR and Asset AccountingSupplier/customer accounting, payment runs, dunning, depreciation, asset acquisition and retirement.
  • 04 · Controlling (CO)Cost centers, profit centers, internal orders, allocations, assessments, distributions and settlement.
  • 05 · Product Costing and production integrationCost estimates, BOM/routing concepts, production and process orders, WIP, variance analysis and settlement.
  • 06 · Integration and S/4HANAFI-MM, FI-SD, Universal Journal, ACDOCA, Material Ledger, COPA and migration considerations.
  • 07 · Period-end close and troubleshootingAccruals, foreign currency valuation, GR/IR, reconciliations, close sequence and incident analysis.
  • 08 · Interview and scenario practiceConfiguration reasoning, business scenarios, error diagnosis and explaining the end-to-end accounting impact.

How to study each topic

For every topic, capture:

  • Business purpose
  • Configuration or process steps
  • Relevant master data
  • Accounting impact
  • Integration points
  • Common errors and fixes
  • Interview questions

Tip: Use a practice company structure and document each configuration decision as you learn.

Core subject areas

Financial Accounting (FI)

GL, AP, AR, Asset Accounting, banking, taxes, ledgers and financial close.

Controlling (CO)

Cost/profit centers, internal orders, product costing, allocations and profitability analysis.

Integration & support

FI-MM, FI-SD, production integration, data migration, testing and support scenarios.
