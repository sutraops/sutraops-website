ABOUT SUTRAOPS

Simplifying Finance, SAP & Business Operations

SutraOps Solutions helps organizations improve finance processes, optimize SAP systems, strengthen controls, and reduce manual effort through practical consulting, process improvement, and automation.

Founded with the belief that businesses need results-driven expertise without the complexity of large consulting firms, we combine deep finance knowledge, SAP experience, and automation capabilities to deliver solutions that improve efficiency, accuracy, and business performance.

ABOUT THE FOUNDER

Fani Bhushan Sharma

Founder, SutraOps Solutions

Fani Bhushan Sharma brings over 11 years of experience in Finance, Accounting, SAP FICO, Process Improvement, and Automation across leading global organizations, including Genpact, Accenture, Wiley, and International Flavors & Fragrances.

His expertise spans SAP FICO, Record-to-Report, General Ledger, Accounts Receivable and Accounts Payable, Intercompany Accounting, Inventory and Cost Accounting, Fixed Assets, Banking and Treasury, Financial Close, and Reporting.

He has supported global finance operations and international project delivery while helping organizations strengthen controls, streamline processes, and improve operational efficiency.

Fani also specializes in process improvement and automation solutions using VBA/VBS, Microsoft Power Automate, and Excel-based tools, enabling businesses to reduce manual work and increase productivity.
