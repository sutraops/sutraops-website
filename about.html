ABOUT SUTRAOPS

Simplifying Finance, SAP & Business Operations

SutraOps Solutions helps organizations improve finance processes, optimize SAP systems, strengthen controls, and reduce manual effort through practical consulting, process improvement, and automation.

Founded with the belief that businesses need results-driven expertise without the complexity of large consulting firms, we combine finance knowledge, SAP experience, and automation capabilities to deliver practical solutions that improve efficiency, accuracy, and business performance.

About the Founder

Fani Bhushan Sharma, Founder of SutraOps Solutions, brings over 11 years of experience in Finance, Accounting, SAP FICO, Process Improvement, and Automation across leading global organizations including Genpact, Accenture, Wiley and International Flavors & Fragrances (IFF).

His expertise spans SAP FICO, Record-to-Report (R2R), General Ledger, Accounts Receivable, Accounts Payable, Intercompany Accounting, Inventory & Cost Accounting, Fixed Assets, Banking & Treasury, Financial Close and Reporting.

He also specializes in automation and process improvement using VBA/VBS, Microsoft Power Automate, and Excel-based solutions, helping organizations eliminate manual work and improve productivity.
